A day of drift scanning

The array stares at the meridian at one declination and lets the sky drift through — a transit telescope. Each frame is a fresh snapshot of your Setup (the same antennas, the same interference) with the real radio sky laid in: the Sun, Cas A, Cyg A, Tau A and Vir A, each drawn only while it is genuinely inside the field.

All times local

The day runs from 00:00 to 24:00 UTC.

Off by default: your sources are placed as offsets from the pointing, so carrying them makes them follow the telescope around all day, which no real source does.
